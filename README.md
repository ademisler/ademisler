Public product surfaces include **[Ternrise](https://ternrise.com)**, **[Carenly](https://carenly.app)**, and **[ChefRise](https://chefrise.app)**. More detailed case studies live on **[ademisler.com/projects](https://ademisler.com/projects/)**.

## What I optimize for

- **Useful before impressive** — narrow products that solve a real workflow clearly.
- **Local-first where it matters** — especially for credentials, machine access, media processing, and personal data.
- **Explicit trust boundaries** — permissions, approvals, auditability, least privilege, and failure modes are product features.
- **Cross-platform pragmatism** — Rust, Swift, Python, TypeScript, Electron, Tauri, web, and mobile depending on the problem.
- **Shipping discipline** — tests, release automation, packaging, security docs, migration paths, and operational runbooks belong in the product.

## Current focus

Right now I am spending most of my technical attention on **AI agent infrastructure, MCP security, local-first desktop software, and production-grade product systems** that connect AI capabilities to real workflows without hiding the operational boundary.
